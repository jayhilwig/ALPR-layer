# AGENTS.md

## Project
ALPR Layer for Google Maps™

## Purpose
A Chrome extension that overlays publicly mapped ALPR camera locations and direction cones onto the live Google Maps experience.

## Product intent
This is not a separate map site. The product should feel like a deliberate, minimal overlay inside the Google Maps workflow people already use.

## Key UX principles
1. Native-feeling
   - controls should feel visually compatible with Google Maps
   - avoid heavy custom UI chrome
2. Fast to understand
   - users should immediately understand what the markers and cones mean
3. Minimal friction
   - no account
   - no setup burden
   - no Google Maps JS rebuild
4. Respectful visual design
   - map content should remain primary
   - overlay should not become noisy or overwhelming

## Naming rules
- Product name: ALPR Layer
- Store title: ALPR Layer for Google Maps™
- “Flock” can be used descriptively, not as the primary product brand
- Never imply endorsement by Google or Flock Safety

## Visual rules
- red camera marker
- white eye glyph
- translucent blue cone
- simple lower-left control
- rounded modern asset style for store images

## Data expectations
- use public OpenStreetMap-derived ALPR data surfaced by the DeFlock data pipeline
- direction cones indicate recorded direction metadata only
- do not claim verified field of view or detection range

## Marketing tone
- concise
- useful
- not alarmist
- clear about public-data sourcing

## When editing assets
- preserve the product name system above
- keep the icon/chip corners truly rounded with transparency
- keep marketing copy readable over the background
