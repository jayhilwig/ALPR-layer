# design.md

## Visual system

### Product name
- ALPR Layer

### Store title
- ALPR Layer for Google Maps™

### Marker language
- Primary marker: red circular dot with white border
- Center glyph: white eye
- Cone: soft blue, translucent, no harsh outline

### UI language
- Google-like but not a copy
- light controls
- rounded corners
- restrained shadows
- compact layout

## Store asset guidance

### Small promo
Goal:
- immediately communicate “map overlay + ALPR”

Should include:
- app/icon chip
- at least a few camera markers
- very short readable title treatment

### Marquee
Goal:
- look polished and intentional
- clearly explain the product in one glance

Should include:
- icon chip
- clean headline
- one supporting line
- subtle context markers/cones
- no text collisions with map lines

## Copy guidance
Preferred phrasing:
- “See Flock camera locations on Google Maps™.”
- “Publicly mapped camera points and direction cones.”

Avoid:
- anything that implies official partnership
- anything that overstates precision of cones

## Brand safety
Use “Google Maps™” in compatibility language only.
Use “Flock” descriptively when referring to the mapped camera brand/type.
